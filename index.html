wei-autopost Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

wei-autopost is a private tool used only by the owner of the Threads account @song_yi_wei_ to manage that account's own posts and replies. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

The owner's own Threads profile and posts, and public Threads posts returned by keyword search (post ID, text, username, link, time).

How we use it

Search results are shown only to the owner in a private review queue. A reply is published only after the owner manually approves it, at most 5 replies per day. No advertising, profiling or automated decisions about other users.

Storage

Data is stored only on the owner's computer. Public posts found by search are deleted after 7 days. Access tokens are never shared.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties.

Data deletion

To have any data about you deleted, email s6811721@gmail.com with the link to your post. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email. You can also revoke access in Threads: Settings, Account, Website permissions.
